Housing disrepair

When repair problems keep coming back, the details matter.

Housing repair issues can range from damp and mould to leaks, heating faults and problems with windows, doors, plumbing or other parts of a property. Keeping a clear record of what is happening, when it was reported and what action was taken can help create a more complete picture of the situation.

This assessment is designed to gather those details in one place. Complete each applicable section as accurately as possible so your information can be reviewed and the appropriate next steps can be explained.

Frequently asked

Questions clients often have.

What information should I provide?

Provide as much accurate information as possible about your property, the repair problems and communications with your landlord.

What problems can I tell you about?

The assessment includes living room, kitchen, hallway, bathroom, bedrooms and external areas, as well as wider repair issues.

What if my landlord has already attempted repairs?

Include details of what was attempted, when it happened and whether the problem remains.

Can I include previous claims?

Yes. The form asks whether you have made a claim before or are currently dealing with another solicitor.
